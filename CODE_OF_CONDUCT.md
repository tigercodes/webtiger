# Code of conduct

WebTiger follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its text applies here in full, to every space the project uses: the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any channel run in the project's name.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise ideas and not people, and accept that the maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break these rules.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers privately. The contact address is published here when the repository is first made public; until then the repository's owner is the contact.

Reports are kept confidential. Nobody is penalised for a report made in good faith.
